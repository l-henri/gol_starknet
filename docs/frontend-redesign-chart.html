Life & status

The creature — live render

Six creatures, actually alive

Each is a real Conway simulation on a 15×15 toroidal grid, rendered from its packed state. Hover or focus any one — it wakes up and steps faster, then settles when you leave. Colour is age, not species. The last one is dead: it leads to an empty grid, so we render it ashen and still — a memento, not a bug.

Motion & cadence

Slow on its own, awake when you lean in

Ambient --step-ambient · 1500ms

Default in the gallery. One generation every ~1.5s, with a soft breathing glow on a 3.6s sine. Calm, peripheral — a garden you can sit with. Still-lifes don't move, so the breath carries them.

Engaged --step-engaged · 160ms

On hover, focus, or the detail page. The interval eases down toward ~160ms so the creature visibly comes to life, then eases back out. The wake and the settle are the gesture.

— freezes stepping and the glow pulse; each creature holds its current state with a single static render. First-class path, not an afterthought.
Provenance fidelity

Same bits, two renders

Our living render is never a different creature — it's the on-chain artifact's exact bits, re-interpreted on a dark, glowing canvas. The detail page offers a toggle between the two, so the provenance is always one tap away.
